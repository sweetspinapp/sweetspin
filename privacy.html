In short: SweetSpin keeps almost everything on your iPhone. The only exception is the leaderboard: your chosen username, treat symbol, streak and points are stored in Apple's iCloud and shown to other players. There are no ads and no tracking.

2. Data stored only on your device

To work, the app stores the following only locally on your iPhone: your chosen difficulty, treat symbol, the number of push-ups you can do in a row, your game progress (spins, streak, flame savers, badges), reps per day and your reminder settings. This data is never sent to us or third parties and is deleted when you delete the app. Part of it (streak and today's status) is shared on your device with the SweetSpin widget.

3. Leaderboard and username

To take part in the leaderboard and the weekly leaderboard you choose a username. Stored and visible to all SweetSpin players are: your username, your treat symbol, your current streak and your points (total and for the current week). A random player ID that does not identify you is stored as well. Please don't pick a name containing your real name if you don't want it to be public.

This data is stored in Apple's public iCloud database (CloudKit). To take part you must be signed in to iCloud on your iPhone; we never receive your Apple ID or email address. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the feature you use). Apple's processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

If another player reports your name as offensive, the report (reported name, player IDs) is stored so we can review it. You can remove your name and points at any time in the app under Settings → Leaderboard → "Remove me from the leaderboard".

4. Camera

The app uses the front camera only when you start an exercise round or the fitness test, to count your reps. Images are analyzed in real time on your device using Apple's body pose detection (Vision). No photos or videos are stored and no image data leaves your device. You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Apple Health (optional)

If you turn it on in Settings, SweetSpin saves each exercise round as a workout in Apple Health (type, time period, number of reps). SweetSpin does not read any data from Health. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app.

6. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your iPhone. No server or device identifier is involved.

7. Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never receive payment details. The app only uses Apple's StoreKit to check whether a subscription is active. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

8. Sharing

When you use "Challenge friends", the app creates an image of your streak on your device. You decide what to share and with whom through the iOS share sheet.

9. No analytics, no ads

SweetSpin contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can delete your leaderboard data yourself in the app; for anything else, email us at the address above. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

11. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects how data is processed, we will update this policy and the date above.
